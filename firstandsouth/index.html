Our corner

Part of Greenport from the get-go

Sarah Phillips Loth and Dan Domingo opened First and South in 2012, in a big Victorian where First Street meets South. It was a neighbourhood place on day one and it has stayed one.

Sarah grew up in Babylon — a small seafaring town where you knew what everyone was having for dinner, and whether their grandma was coming over to eat. That is more or less the room we set out to build: comforting classics, inventive weekly specials, hand-drawn chalkboards, and a bar that keeps its lights on through February as happily as it does through August.

What we are known for

The things people drive out for

House Ground Burgers

Seasoned prime beef, cooked to your temperature, on a toasted potato bun with lettuce, tomato and pickle. A black bean version comes with queso fresco, mango and black garlic aioli.

Triple Blanched First Fries

Blanched three times, fried to order. They come with the burgers, with the mussels, and — most often — on their own, in the middle of the table.

South Street Chowder

Our classic, New England style, with local clams, applewood smoked bacon and fresh herbs.

$13.50 the pint

House-Made Pupusas

Made in-house and served alongside tacos every Tuesday, when the band sets up in the corner and the room fills early.

Sourcing

Grown down the road

We buy seasonal produce locally wherever we can — Deep Roots Farm in Southold and Latham Farms in Orient among them. The specials board follows what turns up, which is why it is rewritten by hand rather than reprinted.
