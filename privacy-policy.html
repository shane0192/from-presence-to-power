Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Rashad Robinson ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website at book.rashadrobinson.com (the "Site"), the companion site for the book From Presence to Power. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit the Site, sign up for updates, or download book extras.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Newsletter sign-up: When you subscribe to email updates, we collect your email address.
  • Appendix notification sign-up: If you request to be notified when the Appendix becomes available, we collect your email address for that purpose.
  • Book exercise downloads: The "Find Your Power" exercises are available as downloadable PDFs. We do not collect personal information when you download them.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics data: We use Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors use the Site, including pages visited, time spent on the Site, browser type, device type, and general geographic location (based on IP address).
  • Cookies: The Site may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to improve site functionality and understand visitor behavior. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect Site functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send newsletter content and book updates to subscribers
  • Notify you when the Appendix becomes available, if you opted in
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve content and navigation
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the Site

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following third-party services, each of which may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Newsletter platform (Kit.com): Email sign-ups and delivery are managed through Kit.com. Their privacy practices are governed by Kit's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: Used to understand Site traffic and visitor behavior. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • reCAPTCHA: Where applicable, forms on this Site may be protected by Google reCAPTCHA to prevent spam. This is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Website hosting (Vercel): This Site is hosted on Vercel, which may collect technical information necessary to deliver the Site. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

4. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of our newsletter at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@rashadrobinson.com.

For California residents (CCPA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

For EU/UK residents (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Our legal basis for processing your information is your consent (newsletter and notification sign-ups).

5. Data Retention

We retain newsletter subscriber information and notification sign-up data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or until you request deletion. You may unsubscribe or request removal at any time.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
